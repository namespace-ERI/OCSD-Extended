MiniHack Room and ScienceWorld native worker and projection source files were
adapted from the author-provided CompeteRL source snapshot retrieved2026-10-15:
https://anonymous.4open.science/r/CompeteRLICLR27-CC5F
That source distributes an Apache-2.0 LICENSE. Changes in this extension include
OCSD-specific manager wiring, explicit split identities, task-type propagation,
ScienceWorld reason-tag compatibility, and new-benchmark action schemas.
ScienceWorld test selection follows examples/data_preprocess/scienceworld.py
from that snapshot (sha256_pair_rank_v1, seed1, size128); the checked-in selection
was generated from its BEACON L0 manifest, not supplied as a paper result file.
MiniHack's checked-in128 test seeds follow the author's preprocessing default;
the PDF omits the exact seed manifest and the launcher instead defaults to64.
MiniHack/NLE and ScienceWorld packages retain their respective upstream licenses;
native simulator binaries and packages are not vendored in this repository.

Skill strategy evidence was extracted using the SkillRL-style prompts adapted
to MiniHack/ScienceWorld from https://github.com/aiming-lab/SkillRL at commit
4d984d455b9b02f0bad283cbe9ab3d66a7e5b89d. Outputs were generated by
glm53_512k_o64k_pd128_coding on Qwen3-4B first-rollout(r00) training trajectories.
The released prompt bank deterministically projects these existing generated
strategy strings; it is not a new model-generated semantic aggregation.
No test or extension-dev trajectory contributes to the released skills.
